# 🎲 Bootstrap Resampling and Phylogenetic Node Support

## 📌 Definition & Theoretical Foundations

In molecular phylogenetics, **Bootstrap Resampling** (introduced by Joseph Felsenstein in 1985) is a statistical non-parametric method used to evaluate the reliability and repeatability of phylogenetic tree topologies.

Because we cannot observe the historical divergence process repeatedly, the multiple sequence alignment of length $N$ sites is treated as an empirical distribution from which pseudo-replicate datasets of length $N$ are generated by **sampling alignment columns with replacement**.

---

## ⚙️ 1. Algorithmic Workflow

```
Original Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA, N sites)
   │
   ├── Repeat B times (typically B = 1,000):
   │     │
   │     ├── 1. Generate Pseudoreplicate Alignment by sampling columns with replacement
   │     ├── 2. Re-estimate Distance Matrix or Likelihood Surface
   │     └── 3. Reconstruct Tree Topology (ML or NJ) -> T_b
   │
   └── Compute Majority-Rule Consensus Tree:
         Count bipartition frequencies (clades) across all B trees.
         Node label = (Count / B) * 100 %
```

### Mathematical Formulation
Given an alignment matrix $\mathbf{X}$ with $M$ taxa and $N$ columns (characters):
$$\mathbf{X} = [\mathbf{c}_1, \mathbf{c}_2, \dots, \mathbf{c}_N]$$
Each bootstrap pseudoreplicate $\mathbf{X}^{(b)}$ is formed by:
$$\mathbf{X}^{(b)} = [\mathbf{c}_{i_1}, \mathbf{c}_{i_2}, \dots, \mathbf{c}_{i_N}], \quad i_k \sim \text{Uniform}\{1, \dots, N\}$$

For any specific clade or bipartition $\beta$:
$$\text{BS}(\beta) = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathbb{I}\left( \beta \in \mathcal{C}(T_b) \right) \times 100\%$$
Where $\mathbb{I}$ is the indicator function and $\mathcal{C}(T_b)$ is the set of all bipartitions present in tree $T_b$.

---

## 🎯 2. Statistical Interpretation & Confidence Thresholds

Phylogenetic literature establishes standard guidelines for interpreting bootstrap values:

* **$\ge 95\%$ — Strong / High Confidence:** Statistically robust clade. Corresponds approximately to a Bayesian posterior probability of $\ge 0.99$ (Felsenstein & Kishino, 1993).
* **$70\% - 94\%$ — Moderate Support:** Likely represents true evolutionary grouping, but may be sensitive to taxon sampling or slight character composition shifts.
* **$< 70\%$ — Weak / Unresolved:** The clade cannot be confidently separated from alternative branching hypotheses (polytomy).
